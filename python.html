PointLayer

Read WGS84 points from a trusted host-selected main-schema relation. The host provisions a tenant-scoped connection and a primary/unique feature_id. Longitudes in the source use the [-180, 180] convention.

  • __init__(self, connection: DuckDBPyConnection, relation: str, *, attributes=None, time_column: str | None=None, catalog: str | None=None)
  • fetch(self, viewport: Viewport, *, where: FilterSpec | None=None, time: TimeRange | None=None) → list[MapPoint]
  • fetch_at_zoom(self, viewport: Viewport, zoom: int, *, where: FilterSpec | None=None, time: TimeRange | None=None) → list[GridCell] | list[MapPoint]

    Use a world-aligned degree grid below zoom 14 and points at higher zoom.

  • polygon_statistics(self, ring: tuple[tuple[float, float], ...], *, sum_attribute: str | None=None) → SelectionStatistics

    Aggregate original point coordinates inside a closed WGS84 selection ring.

GeometryLayer

  • __init__(self, connection: DuckDBPyConnection, relation: str, *, source_crs: str, attributes: Mapping[str, str] | None=None, catalog: str | None=None)
